Hi, I'm **Jiantang Huang** — a research assistant in **Prof. Hang Jiang's Human-AI Nexus Group**, working on LLM agents and reasoning. I hold an MS in Computer and Information Science from Northeastern University and a B.S. in Financial Mathematics (with a CS minor) from The Ohio State University.

My current interests center on **agent-to-agent communication and coordination** — how multiple LLM agents negotiate, share context, and reach joint decisions — alongside **abstract reasoning** (ARC-AGI-style generalization) and **world models** for grounding agent behavior.

Feel free to reach out by email or LinkedIn — I'm always happy to discuss research, collaborations, or new opportunities.
