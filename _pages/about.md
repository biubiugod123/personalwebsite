Hi, I'm **Jiantang Huang** — a Master's student in Computer and Information Science at Northeastern University, graduating in December 2025. I previously earned a B.S. in Financial Mathematics (with a CS minor) from The Ohio State University.

My current interests sit at the intersection of **large language models, reasoning, and multimodal perception**. Recent work includes chain-of-thought distillation for instruction-tuned LLMs (SFT vs. DPO/ORPO on Flan-T5), a Gemini-powered AI dance coaching system using RAG, and an experimental framework for analyzing social-boundary failures in LLM-based agents. I also published research on **slow-motion video synthesis for basketball using frame interpolation** ([arXiv:2511.11644](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11644)).

I work in **Python, C/C++, Go, Java**, and Bash, and routinely use TensorFlow, PyTorch, OpenCV, MLflow, Docker, and Azure. I'm Microsoft Certified in Azure AI Fundamentals.

Feel free to reach out by email or LinkedIn — I'm always happy to discuss research, collaborations, or new opportunities.
